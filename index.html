HOW TO PLAY

  • Build a squad of 5 different heroes on the Home screen. Save up to 8 squads (Save as…, Load, Rename, Delete); the green one is your active squad.
  • 🤖 Pick the AI level: Easy (random squad, simple plays), Normal (smart squad, weighs every move), Hard (plans two moves ahead).
  • Both HQs start with 20 HP. Knock the enemy HQ to 0 to win!
  • You hold up to 2 cards; the NEXT card is shown on the right. When you play a hero, the next card in your cycle fills its slot. A hero on the board stays out of the cycle until it is knocked out, then it goes to the back of the cycle. With 4+ heroes on the board your hand may run short (empty slots), so trade wisely!
  • ⚡ Escalating energy: each turn your max Energy grows and refills. Rounds 1–5: +1 per turn · rounds 6–10: +2 · round 11+: +3 (no cap). A POWER SURGE! banner shows when your rate goes up. Max 4 heroes on the board.
  • You go first; the villains get +2 bonus ⚡ on their first turn to even it out.
  • ⏰ Overtime & time limit: from round 10, each HQ takes storm damage at the start of its turn: 2, then 4, 6, 8… The match ends after round 20: the HQ with more HP wins (equal HP = draw). Watch the Round X/20 counter.
  • Heroes can attack the turn after they're played (unless Swift). Tap your hero, then an enemy hero or the enemy HQ, to fight in the arena.
  • 🌐 Online 1v1: tap Play Online, enter your name, then Host to get a 5-letter code (share it) or type a friend's code and Join. Each player uses their own active squad; the host goes first. Leaving the match counts as a forfeit. Some strict networks (work/school Wi-Fi, some mobile carriers) may block the direct connection.
  • Hero vs hero: both deal their Power to each other. Attacking the HQ takes no damage back.
  • If the enemy has a Taunt 🛡️‍ hero, you must attack it first.
  • Abilities: 🛡 Shield blocks the first hit · 💚 Heal restores HQ HP on play · ⚔️ Double Strike attacks twice · 💫 Stun freezes the target for its next turn · 🏃 Swift attacks right away · 🧱 Taunt (only while the hero is undamaged) · 🩸 Drain heals your HQ by damage dealt · 💥 Blast zaps an enemy on play · 📣 Rally gives allies +1 Power on play.
